1. Introduction

Welcome to Billora — Simple Invoice Maker ("Billora", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by NH TechLabs.

We believe that your business and financial records belong strictly to you. Billora is designed from the ground up as an offline-first mobile invoice application. We do not operate user accounts, we do not require a sign-in, and we do not operate remote application servers to collect, store, or monetize your business records.

This Privacy Policy explains in plain, simple language what data is handled when you use Billora on your Android device, where that data is stored, and your rights regarding your information.

2. Information Handled by Billora

When you use Billora to run your daily billing operations, you enter information necessary to create and manage invoices. This includes:

  • Business Profile Information: Your store or shop name, proprietor name, business phone number, business email address, shop physical address, business logo, tax identification number (optional), and custom invoice terms or footer greetings.
  • Customer Information: Customer names and contact phone numbers entered on invoices.
  • Transaction and Invoice Details: Sequential invoice numbers, dates, line items (item descriptions, quantities, unit prices, discounts), subtotal amounts, applicable taxes, grand totals, amounts paid, payment methods (e.g., cash, bank transfer, card), payment statuses (paid, partial, unpaid), outstanding balances, and invoice notes.
  • Local Preferences: Currency symbol and code (e.g., Rs. / PKR), paper slip width preference (e.g., 80mm or 58mm), and UI settings.

3. Local Data Storage & Android System Backup

All business and customer records you enter into Billora are stored locally on your device inside a private SQLite database (billora.db) managed by the Android operating system sandbox.

Key Guarantee: Billora operates as an offline-first mobile invoicing tool and does not operate its own remote servers, cloud databases, or backend synchronization services to collect or store your business records. Although the Android package includes the standard baseline INTERNET permission, Billora's application code performs zero network requests and does not transmit your records to NH TechLabs or any external party.
  • No Proprietary Cloud Database: Billora does not operate remote database servers to store or process your customer records, transaction details, or store settings.
  • Offline Functionality: The application functions completely without requiring mobile data or Wi-Fi.
  • Operating System Sandbox: Android's application sandbox isolates your SQLite database (/data/user/0/com.billora.app/databases/) from access by other standard applications on your device.
  • Automatic Operating System Backup Disabled: To ensure maximum privacy and guarantee that your business data never leaves your device without your explicit action, Billora explicitly disables automatic Android cloud backup (android:allowBackup="false"). The operating system will not automatically copy your SQLite database to Google Drive in the background. Backups are created only when you manually initiate them.

4. Customer and Invoice Information

Customer names, phone numbers, and transaction records entered into Billora are used solely on your device to generate invoices, calculate sales summaries, and track payment statuses.

Strict Separation of Data Responsibilities:

  • No Collection or Sharing by NH TechLabs: NH TechLabs does not collect, receive, store, rent, sell, or monetize your customer records or transaction history. No customer details are ever transmitted to NH TechLabs. We do not access your device's contacts list, and we do not build marketing profiles of your customers.
  • Voluntary, User-Initiated Sharing: When you choose to send an invoice or receipt to a customer, accountant, or business partner, you voluntarily initiate that sharing action through Android's native system share menu (e.g., via WhatsApp, Email, Messaging, or Drive). NH TechLabs is not a party to this transfer, has no access to the transmitted document, and cannot view or retain copies of files you share with external applications.

5. PDF Generation and Sharing

Billora includes features that allow you to generate professional PDF invoices and receipts:

  • Local Document Creation: Invoices are rendered directly on your phone as standard PDF documents using your device's built-in print renderer.
  • Temporary Storage: Generated PDF files are temporarily held in your application's secure document cache.
  • System Share Menu Handoff: When you tap "Share Invoice", Billora delegates the file handoff to the Android operating system using a secure Content Provider (FileSystemFileProvider).
  • User Discretion & Control: The file is sent only to the specific external application (such as WhatsApp, Gmail, or a file manager) that you actively choose in the system dialog. You maintain full discretion over when, where, and with whom your business documents are shared.

6. Backup and Restore

To safeguard against device damage or hardware upgrades, Billora provides a local-first backup system with optional user-initiated cloud backup:

A. Local Backup (Default)

  • Local-First Creation: When you tap Create Local Backup in Settings, Billora generates an unencrypted, structured JSON file (Billora-Backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json) containing your store profile, invoice sequences, invoices (including soft-deleted records), and line items.
  • On-Device Storage Destination: The file is saved directly into a local device folder you choose via Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) (such as your Downloads folder or an SD card).
  • Guaranteed Confinement: Local backups are stored strictly on your device. Billora will never automatically upload them to the cloud.

B. Optional User-Initiated Cloud Backup (Google Drive / External Location)

  • User-Initiated Action Only: If you wish to export a backup copy to Google Drive or another external location, you can explicitly tap Share / Export Backup Copy in Settings.
  • System Document Provider Delegation: Billora prepares the backup file and hands it off to Android's system document provider / sharing menu (ACTION_SEND). You can then select Google Drive ("Save to Drive") or another available document provider (such as OneDrive or Dropbox) as the destination.
  • Zero Third-Party APIs or Credentials: Billora does not connect to the Google Drive REST API, does not use Google OAuth, and does not collect or store your Google account credentials or tokens. The transfer is handled directly by Android and the destination app you choose.
  • No Background Sync: No backup is ever created, uploaded, or synchronized automatically in the background.

C. Backup Restoration

  • User-Directed File Selection: When you tap Restore from Backup, Android's system file picker opens, allowing you to select a backup file stored locally on your device or in your Google Drive account.
  • Scoped Access: Billora accesses only the specific backup file you select.
  • Validation & Atomic Transaction: The file is fully validated for structural integrity, version compatibility, and data safety before being imported. If valid, the database is restored inside an atomic SQLite transaction. If validation fails or you cancel, your current local database remains 100% untouched.
  • User Security Responsibility: Because backup files contain unencrypted business records, you are solely responsible for safeguarding backup files stored on your device or in your cloud storage accounts.

7. Third-Party Applications and Services

We maintain a clean, tracker-free environment:

  • No Advertising SDKs: Billora does not contain third-party advertising SDKs or ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity, or AppLovin).
  • No Analytics or Telemetry SDKs: Billora does not include behavioral tracking or analytics frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or Mixpanel).
  • No Crash Reporting Trackers: Billora does not send automated background crash or performance reports to external tracking platforms.
  • Android OS Permissions: Billora does not request sensitive permissions such as Camera, Contacts, Microphone, Precise Location, or Bluetooth hardware access. Legacy storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) are explicitly blocked via Expo configuration. While baseline Android application containers declare the standard INTERNET permission, Billora's application code performs zero network requests and transmits no data over the internet.

8. Data Security and Limitations

We care about protecting your business data, and we are transparent about how security works in Billora:

  • No Remote Application Server: NH TechLabs operates no remote servers, web APIs, or cloud infrastructure to receive, process, or store your business records.
  • No Application-Level Automated Transmission: Billora's application code performs zero background telemetry and makes zero outbound network calls.
  • Automatic OS Backup Disabled: Android's automated operating-system backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false"). No background backup transfers occur. Backups saved to Google Drive or external locations are initiated solely and explicitly by you via the Android document chooser. NH TechLabs has no access to, visibility into, or control over files stored in your personal Google Account.
  • Unencrypted Local Files: The on-device SQLite database (billora.db) and exported manual JSON backups (Billora-Backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json) are stored in unencrypted format.
  • Operating System Sandboxing: Android isolates Billora's application directory from other installed applications through standard Linux user permissions.
User Security Responsibility: Because local files are unencrypted, the application does not make absolute or infallible security guarantees. Users are responsible for protecting their physical devices with a secure screen lock (PIN, password, or biometric authentication), safeguarding exported backup files, and configuring device backup settings according to their own business security standards.

9. Data Retention, Soft Deletion, and Permanent Removal

You retain full ownership of your records. Please understand how data retention, deletion, and cloud backups operate within Billora:

  • Soft Deletion in SQLite: When you delete an invoice within the application, it is soft-deleted by setting is_deleted = 1. It is immediately removed from active invoice lists, customer ledgers, and dashboard calculations. However, the underlying record remains stored in your local SQLite database to preserve sequence number continuity and transaction audit history. Deleting an invoice inside the app does not permanently erase its row from the database. Soft-deleted records are included in backup exports.
  • Permanent Local Erasure: You can permanently destroy your active local SQLite database, settings, and cached documents on your phone at any time by clearing application data in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Billora > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage) or by uninstalling the application. Because automatic OS cloud backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false"), clearing local storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all active business data from your device.
  • Managing and Deleting Exported Backups: Any backup files (Billora-Backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json) or PDF invoices you manually saved to your device storage (such as Downloads or Documents) or copied to Google Drive remain under your direct control in those locations even after Billora is uninstalled. You can delete these files at any time directly through your phone's file manager or the Google Drive application. NH TechLabs operates no servers and has no ability to access or delete files stored in your local storage or personal Google Drive account.
